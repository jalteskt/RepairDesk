Need a Repair?

Every repair starts with a hands-on physical inspection at our counter — it's how we quote accurately before any work begins.

  • 1 Message us

    Tell us what's broken — appliance type, brand, and the fault you're seeing. We'll get back to you quickly.
  • 2 Bring the unit in

    Physical inspection is essential — drop your unit at the counter so a technician can diagnose it properly.
  • 3 Approve the quote

    After inspection you receive a written quote. Approve it and the repair starts; the diagnostic fee is credited to the job.
