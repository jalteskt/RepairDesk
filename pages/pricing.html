Shop Policies

Payment Methods

We accept Cash, GCash, Maya, and Credit/Debit Cards (Visa/Mastercard) at our Gilmore counter. A downpayment may be required for parts orders exceeding ₱5,000.

30-Day Collection Window

Completed repairs must be claimed within 30 days of our "Ready for Pickup" notification. A storage fee of ₱150/day applies from Day 31. Units unclaimed after 1 year may be liquidated to offset costs.

Parts & Labor Warranty

All completed repairs carry a 90-day warranty on replaced parts and workmanship. Warranty is void if the unit is tampered with, subjected to liquid damage, or physical impact after repair.

Written Quote Approval

No repair work begins without your explicit approval. You will receive a detailed written quote via SMS or email with an itemized cost breakdown. You can approve or decline through our online customer portal.

Diagnostic Fee Policy

The diagnostic fee is charged regardless of whether you proceed with the repair. If you approve the quote, the diagnostic fee is applied toward your total bill. If declined, only the diagnostic fee is due upon item collection.

Beyond Economic Repair

If our technicians determine that repair cost exceeds the unit's market value, we will advise you accordingly. Only the diagnostic fee is collected. We will assist in responsible unit disposal or parts recovery if requested.
