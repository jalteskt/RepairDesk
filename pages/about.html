Our Story

RepairDesk was born out of frustration. Our founders operated a bustling electronics repair shop along Gilmore Avenue in Quezon City and were buried in carbon-copy paper tickets, missed customer calls, and misplaced units. Every day was hectic — and existing systems were either too rigid or not designed for local repair shops.

So they built RepairDesk. What started as an internal tool to track intake items, diagnostic approvals, and technician queues quickly became the system that repair shops across Metro Manila, Cebu, and Davao rely on every single day.

Today, our team is united by one mission: to make repair workshop management seamless, transparent, and trusted for technicians and customers alike.
